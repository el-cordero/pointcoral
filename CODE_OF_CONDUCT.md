# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Participants should welcome questions asked in good faith, focus
criticism on ideas and evidence, respect privacy and data-use restrictions, and
acknowledge uncertainty in scientific and technical discussions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers
may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer at
elvin.cordero@seamountgeo.com. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when a
person is publicly representing the project.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
